About Simple Tumble

Simple Tumble is a rolling cube puzzle generator for Unity, made by Living Failure. It builds puzzles where a cube tips from tile to tile and has to land the right face down on the goal, or press it onto every target, on square or shaped boards with keys and doors, switches and gates, avoid and one-way tiles, multi-face tiles, turntables, crumbling tiles and portals. Then it solves each puzzle exactly, stamps it with its proven par and a 1 to 5 star difficulty, and stores it as a ScriptableObject or exports it as a picture. A complete 3D demo, built entirely in code, is included.

Version 1.0, made and tested with Unity 6000.6 in URP, HDRP and the Built-in pipeline.

Design principles

  • Solve exactly. The par, the number of best solutions and whether the cube can get stuck come from searching every position a puzzle can reach, not from an estimate.
  • Measure, then promise. The star bands, the presets, Auto's fixes and the demo's difficulty recipes all come from measuring real puzzles, hundreds at a time, and the docs say so where it matters.
  • Be honest. When a request can't be met, the puzzle's notes say so. Nothing is quietly downgraded, and the store page claims nothing the solver can't back up.
  • Every gimmick matters. A key, a switch or a tile that changes nothing about the puzzle isn't left on the board.
  • One set of rules. The generator, the solver and the play rules your game uses all roll the cube the same way, so your game and the par always agree.
  • Not by colour alone. Every face has a shape as well as a colour, and every gimmick its own shape, in the pictures and in the demo, so colour-blind players can play every puzzle.
  • No bloat. The core has no package dependencies, and the demo and the editor tools live in their own assemblies. The demo needs no prefabs, models, textures or audio files.

More from Living Failure

Simple Color Sort: ball sort and water sort puzzles with up to 15 colours, locked tubes and hidden pieces, each solved exactly and rated for difficulty and traps.

Simple Maze: mazes on square, hex, triangle and circular grids, with keys and doors, switches, gems, portals, rolling players and up to 8 floors, each solved exactly and rated.

Simple Klotski: sliding-block puzzles in five modes (Gridlock, Klotski, Clear, Clear 3D and Clear Snakes), each board solved exactly and stamped with its proven par.

Simple States: switch-and-state logic puzzles in four modes: Classic combinations, Ordered chains with traps, Lights Out with paintable pattern goals, and the three-state Trilight grid.

Simple Connect: a connection-puzzle toolkit where you draw the pairs (square and hex), rotate the pipes, or rotate a whole network back together.

Simple Hamiltonian: a cover-all, single-path puzzle generator (square and hex), where the solution is one unbroken line that visits every tile once.

All on the Unity Asset Store.

Support

Questions, bugs and ideas are welcome at livingfailuregames@gmail.com. Please include your Unity version and, for anything about a particular puzzle, its seed and settings, since a seed rebuilds the puzzle exactly. Corrections to these pages are welcome too.
